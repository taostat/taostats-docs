> 📘 Recycled vs. burned
>
> **Recycled tao/alpha** just becomes unissued - ready to be emitted again at a later date
>
> **Burned tao/alpha** is destroyed. This token no longer exists, and cannot be used.

# Tao

* Tao extrinsic fees are burned.

# Alpha

* Mining emission to the subnet owner hotkey is, by default, burned. (it can be recycled)
* Subnet owners can burn alpha through an extrinsic call.
