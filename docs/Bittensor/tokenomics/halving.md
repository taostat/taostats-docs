The [tokenomics](https://taostats.io/tokenomics)  of Bittensor follows that of Bitcoin. Upon issuance of half of the remaining supply, the emission will halve.

The tokenomics are the same for tao and for all alpha tokens.

## First Halving

When 10,500,000 tokens are issued, the block reward will drop by 50%. Tao and alpha halvings will occur on different schedules based on their emission.

* **tao**:
  * When 10.5M tao have been issued, tao emission will drop 1 to 0.5
* **alpha**:
  * When 10.5M alpha have been issued, alpha emission will drop 2 to 1

> 📘 First Halving date for tao
>
> The halving date cannot be accurately predicted, as recycling causes tao to be taken out of circulation.
>
> 1/14/25 data:\
> Assuming no recycle: Halving will be December 4, 2025.
>
> Based on last 30 days of registration recycle fees: between December 11 - 21, 2025

> 📘 The tao halving will affect the timing for alpha halvings.
>
> Recall that alpha into the pool is based on the amount of tao entering the pool.  When tao halves, the amount of tao entering each pool will halve - and so will the alpha injected into the pool.
>
> The tao halving is shown by the yellow line. At this time, alpha\_in decreases- changing the slope of the emission line.
>
> The red dotted line indicates 10.5M alpha- the first halving.
