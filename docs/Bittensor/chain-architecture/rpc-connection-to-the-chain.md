How do you extract knowledge from the Bittensor chain? They [taostats API](https://docs.taostats.io/reference/welcome-to-the-taostats-api#/)  gets you 99.9% of the way there.

There are some calls that may require direct access into the chain.  There is a [Python SDK](https://github.com/opentensor/bittensor) for some direct access.
