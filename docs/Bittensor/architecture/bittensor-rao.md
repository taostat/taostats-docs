# New Terms

`btcli subnet show`: the new "metagraph" 

**Slippage**: change in price that will occur during the transfer.  High slippage means initial loss of tao.

**alpha** Each subnet has a token corresponding to it's symbol (alpha, beta, delta....). To simplify, all subnet tokens are referred to as alpha.

**Lock** When registering a subnet, a portion of the tao is locked. This is the way that subnet owners retain control of the subnet.
