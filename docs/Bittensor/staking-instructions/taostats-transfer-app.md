The [Taostats Transfer](https://dash.taostats.io/transfer/transfer-alpha)  page makes switching your validator easy.

1. With a [Connected wallet](doc:connecting-your-wallet), choose the subnet you wish to swap

   <Image align="center" width="25% " src="https://files.readme.io/5d9157073a5f36c56a5e45bd4671e429adb69c2a857d5849141ba64568e2f3a4-image.png" />
2. Enter the amount to swap

<Image align="center" width="75% " src="https://files.readme.io/df5508f84b39c8f3d25e6688a243589e7327d46a95d8b4120b9132dd9e02de84-image.png" />

3. Click "Use Source Wallet" to place your coldkey in the field:

   ![](https://files.readme.io/cea0c4796463897d52f7b149832a45350d294b19183035273508e289904cd862-image.png)
4. Select the new validator.  You can enter a manual hotkey if desired
5. ![](https://files.readme.io/83b45dc98e9379171eff8b14488321d9879190e3344d744b09cb5455f0245d6c-image.png)

   When all looks good, click Confirm and enter your wallet passowrd.  The transfer of your stake to the new validator will be completed on chain.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/a5affc6dd1bf032a018fe98f4e23ea5dec4801585c788dad6d6a0a5dd9348986-image.png)
