To delegate with the taostats apps, you must:

<br />

1. have a wallet that has a browser extension
2. Click Connect Wallet to add a wallet:
3. ![](https://files.readme.io/21cc3bc5349d734857e95bd7b23e60178c0965067b26cde26792051e76658dd4-image.png)

   Your wallet extension will ask for permission to connect to taostats (Polkadot wallet is shown)
4. ![](https://files.readme.io/7515bec5f1f232d1d92c145215e137939c221d27ee319b90cff8eb16d3e4d5f8-image.png)
5. Click connect and enter your password
