taostats.io is a full-featured blockchain explorer and portfolio manager for Bittensor. This section explains every feature of the product itself — how to buy TAO, manage an account, browse subnets, stake, validate, mine, and use Pro tools.

## Pick where to start

	## Where to buy TAO

Exchanges, on-ramps, and how to acquire TAO before you can stake, validate, or transact.

	[Where to buy →](/docs/where-to-buy)

	## Accounts

Coldkey and hotkey basics, single-account view, sending and transferring, delegation, staking instructions, the staking and transfer apps, limit orders, and troubleshooting.

	[Accounts →](/docs/accounts)

	## Subnets

Browse subnets on taostats.io, use the single-subnet page, subnet pools, sentiment, deregistration ranking, and top-subnets-by-emission.

	[Subnets →](/docs/subnets)

	## Validators

The validator explorer — single-validator page, performance history, and validator-alpha-shares breakdown.

	[Validators →](/docs/validators)

	[Miners →](/docs/taostats-for-miners)

Block explorer, event stream, extrinsic search, transfers, and the underlying substrate chain data.

	[Chain →](/docs/blocks)

	## Pro

Portfolio tracking, tax reports, alerts, the Pro API, MEV shield, and every feature that's part of taostats.io Pro.

	[Pro →](/docs/portfolio)
