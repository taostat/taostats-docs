The **coldkey view** on Taostats shows all activity tied to a single coldkey in one place — every hotkey, stake position, and balance owned by that account.

> 📘 This page is being expanded. For the full account breakdown it feeds into, see the [Single account page](/docs/single-account-page).

## Key points

* A **coldkey** is the secure, offline-held key that owns balances and stakes; a **hotkey** is the online key used for validating or mining.
* The coldkey view aggregates every hotkey under a coldkey — useful for seeing all your miners or validators at once.
* Drill into any individual hotkey from here to reach its detailed [hotkey view](/docs/hotkey-page).
