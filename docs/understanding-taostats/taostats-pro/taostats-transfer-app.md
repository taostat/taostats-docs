Swap validators on a subnet with the taostats transfer tool.

The [Taostats Transfer](https://taostats.io/pro/transfer/transfer-alpha)  page makes switching your validator easy.

1. With a [Connected wallet](/docs/connecting-your-wallet), choose the subnet you wish to swap

   <Image border={false} alt="Taostats transfer app with an open subnet selector dropdown listing subnets by icon, ID, and name to choose the one to swap" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/e8646a7e310e4cbd.png" />
2. Enter the amount to swap

<Image border={false} alt="Taostats transfer app amount field with a numeric input, a token selector, an available balance readout, and a Max button" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/156b861227aa6b8c.png" />

3. Click "Use Source Wallet" to place your coldkey in the field:

   <Image border={false} alt="Taostats transfer app To Address field with a wallet icon and a Use Source Wallet button to auto-fill the coldkey address" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/0dd0e440121ab1bc.png" />
4. Select the new validator.  You can enter a manual hotkey if desired
5. <Image border={false} alt="Taostats transfer app From Validator and To Validator selectors with a dropdown and an enter-manual-hotkey option for choosing the new validator" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/b419a2aeeb697b71.png" />

   When all looks good, click Confirm and enter your wallet password.  The transfer of your stake to the new validator will be completed on chain.

   <Image border={false} alt="Taostats transfer alpha form with amount, to address, from and to validator fields and a confirm button to complete the stake transfer on chain" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/7947364f5fdfbb79.png" />
