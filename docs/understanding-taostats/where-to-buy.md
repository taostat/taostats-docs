TAO is listed on a range of centralized and decentralized exchanges. See [taostats.io/exchanges](https://taostats.io/exchanges) for the current, canonical list — each card links straight to the exchange's TAO market.

<Image border={false} alt="Taostats where-to-buy page showing a grid of clickable exchange cards, each with a logo, name, and external-link icon" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/7744ad54eae60879.png" />

Once you hold TAO, you can [connect your wallet](/docs/connecting-your-wallet) and [stake it](/docs/taostats-for-staking) to start earning.
