Staking is how TAO holders earn yield on Bittensor. Under **dTao** you can stake to **root** (TAO only, no price risk) or to a **subnet** (buying that subnet's alpha token, higher risk and reward). Start with [Staking in dTao](/docs/staking-in-dtao) for the full model, then dig into price impact, root baskets, and yield.
