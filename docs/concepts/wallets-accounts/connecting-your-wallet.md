Taostats' staking tools require a connection with a wallet extension.

To delegate with the taostats apps, you must:

1. have a wallet that has a browser extension
2. Click Connect Wallet to add a wallet:
3. <Image border={false} alt="Dark-themed app panel with a light Connect Wallet button bearing a wallet icon and a Buy action below" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/6592d2f3ca07f7c6.png" />

   Your wallet extension will ask for permission to connect to taostats (Polkadot wallet is shown)
4. <Image border={false} alt="Polkadot.js account connection request dialog showing the requesting app and URL, an account selection list with identicons, and Connect and Reject buttons" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taostat/taostats-docs/v5.0/docs-assets/img/e4605d0b76b5f0ec.png" />
5. Click connect and enter your password
