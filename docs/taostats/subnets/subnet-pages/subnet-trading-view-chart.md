Each subnet has a trading view chart: showing the price of alpha over time.  These charts are easily configurable, with many different options.

<br />

The default time for each point is 1 hour:

<br />

![](https://files.readme.io/24edbadda0894b6a86a1fc3e8671192080369982b47172cc1378e1ae4deae2b5-image.png)

The display can be updated

<br />

![](https://files.readme.io/a8f340ed61c21e0e09cad65e2b5485bf6a3535a4cf293c57e445bb9d45937323-image.png)

Your settings will be saved, and repeated across all subnets.
