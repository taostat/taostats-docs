The top of the Blockchain page details market information about the Bittensor blockchain. 

# Accounts

The chart shows the growth of accounts in the Bittensor blockchain.  Orange indicates all accounts, and the blue indicates how many accounts are non-zero.

![](https://files.readme.io/6d50db2-image.png)

# Issuance

A second tab on the chart switches to a graph of TAO issuance. The orange line shows the total issued TAO, and the blue line denotes how much of the issued TAO is staked. Rate is simply the percentage of existing TAO that is staked.

![](https://files.readme.io/3113c0f-image.png)
